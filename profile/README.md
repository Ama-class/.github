### Kirokune — workplace incident log

Log what happens at work as it happens: a note, a voice recording or a photo, kept in one dated timeline. You can also import chat screenshots and message history, so scattered evidence ends up in a single record you can explain later.

- Records are stored on the device. Entry text, audio, names and case names are never sent for analytics.
- Built for people who do not want to be seen using it: a discreet display mode and quick capture from a widget.
- No account required.

[Website](https://kirokune.com/en/) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id6799403273)

`Swift` `SwiftUI` `WidgetKit` `StoreKit`

### SHIBA3 (しばしば芝) — lawn care log and community

Record mowing, watering and fertilizing in one tap, keep photos of how the lawn changes, and see when the next task is due. Share progress with other lawn owners, or keep everything private. An AI assistant reads a photo and helps put a vague worry ("it is turning brown") into words.

[Website](https://shiba3.com) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id6777942807) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amaclass.shiba)

`Expo` `React Native` `Supabase` `Next.js` `microCMS`

### Lantern Hill — a companion for everyday parenting

For everyday life with children 12 and under. Start with what is on your mind today, find a practical idea to try, choose a little play together, and keep a few words to return to later. Notes on strengths, difficult moments and what you have tried can be gathered into a plain-text summary for a conversation with preschool, school or a clinician.

- Parenting articles on the site can be read without the app.
- The site is available in English, Japanese, Spanish, German, French, Brazilian Portuguese, Korean and Traditional Chinese.
- The app is being prepared for public release. Pre-registration is open.

[Website](https://lanternhill.app)

### Karuttona! (カルっとな！) — visit notes for clinicians

Transcribes the conversation during a visit, separates the speakers and assembles a SOAP draft. Recording, transcription and draft generation all run on the device.

- Audio, transcripts and drafts are not sent to our servers. Data stored on the device is encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
- It does not diagnose, suggest treatment or judge urgency. Every output is a draft for a licensed clinician to check against the original audio.
- A Chrome extension for telemedicine, Karuttona for Web, is being prepared as a limited beta.

[Website](https://karuttona.com)

`Swift` `SwiftUI` `On-device speech recognition`

### Daily 1% (毎日1%改善) — daily website improvements

Reads your search data and your pages, then sends up to three improvement proposals on mornings when there is something worth changing. Each proposal shows the text before and after, ready to copy. On days without a good proposal, nothing is sent.

Sites in any country can apply, and the site is available in English and other languages.

[Website](https://get1pct.com/en) · [For clinics](https://www.ama-class.com/clinic/1pct)

## How we work

- **Listen to the people doing the work first.** We start from the business context and the daily problems, not from a specification.
- **Build small and check together.** Screens and prototypes say more than documents.
- **Plan for what comes after launch.** Operation, handover and ongoing improvement are part of the design.
- **Decide what to protect at the start.** Where data is stored, what leaves the device, who can see it, and where a person reviews AI output.
